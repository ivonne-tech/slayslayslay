Proposition 209 was passed in California in 1996. It banned public institutions from considering race or gender in admissions, hiring, and financial aid decisions. Supporters argued the policy promoted equal treatment, while opponents believed it ignored existing inequalities in education and society. At UCSB and other campuses, students organized protests, walkouts, and boycotts because they feared the policy would decrease diversity and access for underrepresented communities.
