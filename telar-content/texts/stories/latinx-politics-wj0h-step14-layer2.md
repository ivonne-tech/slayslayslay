title: "Go deeper"
---

All stories demonstrate different forms of resistance against policies and attitudes that many Chicano students believed threatened their educational opportunities and cultural representation. While students protested through direct action, scholars like Acuña defended these same goals through research, writing, and public criticism. Together, they reveal that social change often requires both grassroots activism and intellectual advocacy.

These events are also connected to Latinx politics because they show ongoing struggles for representation, equity, and inclusion. Although this project focuses primarily on Chicano activism, the issues these students raised affected Latino communities and contributed to conversations about access to higher education across California and beyond. Their efforts show how local activism can influence broader political discussions about race, education, and opportunity.
